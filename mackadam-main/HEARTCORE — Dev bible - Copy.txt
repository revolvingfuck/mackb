4.4 Luto's Balance System
Balance Value	Paladin Effectiveness	Berserker Effectiveness	Berserker Cost
0 (Alone)	50%	150%	50 Remorse/sec
1	70%	130%	40 Remorse/sec
2	85%	115%	30 Remorse/sec
3 (Held)	100%	100%	20 Remorse/sec
Balance Formula: Average party interiority ÷ 2 (rounded down)

Avg 0-1 → Balance 0

Avg 2-3 → Balance 1

Avg 4-5 → Balance 2

Avg 6 → Balance 3

Berserker activation: 100 Remorse minimum. Drains 20-50 Remorse/sec.
Paladin activation: Free. Available always.
