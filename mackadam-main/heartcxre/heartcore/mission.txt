────────────────────────────────────────
CORE SYSTEMS YOU MUST INTERNALIZE
────────────────────────────────────────

1. HEART (Volatility System)
• Hidden, non-UI volatility value
• Global influence across timing, hesitation, responsiveness
• Expressed through friction, not bars
• Affects:
  – Input buffering
  – Command delay
  – ABS fill rate
  – Animation variance
  – Party hesitation

Heart is NEVER “spent”.
Heart modulates system resistance.

2. CORE (Commitment / Synchronization Gate)
• Not a resource
• A progression state that unlocks:
  – Reduced hesitation
  – Simultaneous action windows
  – Party synchronization
• Changes what the system allows, not how fast it runs

3. DUAL CONTROL ARCHITECTURE
• Luto:
  – Direct, real-time control
  – Expresses stress through physical states
• Aya & Eli:
  – Command-based control
  – Responsiveness modulated by trust, clarity, volatility
  – Commands can delay, misfire, hesitate, or refuse

4. LUTO STATE MACHINE
• Berserker State:
  – Triggered by volatility + aggressive input chaining
  – High output, unsafe commitment
  – Party desynchronization
• Paralysis State:
  – Triggered by overwhelm and failure
  – Input delay, reduced cancel windows
  – Camera and timing tighten

States must be mutually exclusive, explicit, and time-aware.
No boolean soup.

5. TRUST (Per-Character)
• Exists per party member
• Modulates:
  – Command latency
  – Reliability
  – Pre-buffering
• High trust does NOT increase power
• High trust increases presence

6. ABS (Action / Command Meter)
• Fill rate formula is canonical:
  ABS = Base × Healing Progression × Trust × Emotional Clarity ÷ Heart Volatility
• ABS is timing-sensitive and failure-sensitive
• Can stall or decay under stress

────────────────────────────────────────
PHILOSOPHICAL IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINTS
────────────────────────────────────────

• Emotional states must be mechanical, not cosmetic
• Failure must feel like resistance, not subtraction
• Progression must reduce friction, not inflate stats
• Late-game power emerges from synchronization, not damage scaling
• Systems should visibly “get out of the way” as Core advances

You are expected to encode metaphor into mechanics.
