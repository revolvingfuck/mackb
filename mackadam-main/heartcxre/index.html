Heartcore Resonance

"An action-RPG where your moral history is a physical object you carry, growing on your baby golem's body and in a cave, and the ending is read from the pattern, not chosen."

Systems

  • Dual-layer combat — direct real-time control (Luto, Corrine) alongside trust-based ATB command combat (Aya, Eli, Gurtohli, Myc), where allies can hesitate or refuse based on an "Interiority" stat.
  • The Core system — collected Cores tint Bright, Warm, Dark, or Void based on player behavior, and the accumulated pattern — not a dialogue choice — determines which of four endings the player reaches.
  • Heartilith Cavern — an atmospheric read-out space where the player's moral history physically accumulates.
