Story Timeline

Act I: First Contact

Luto navigates back alleys and rooftops, avoiding gang members on his way home to his rooftop hideout. Upon arrival, he discovers a message waiting for him from an unknown source asking him to go to the ruins in the mountain forest.

Meeting Aya

Luto makes his way to the forest and discovers Aya, who runs from him. Player Choice: Chase her or wait around for her to return (first trust building moment). Aya chooses to return to the hideout with Luto, who promises to keep her safe.

Recruiting Eli

The next day, Luto and Aya head back to the forest. On the way they pass through the slums/junkyard where Eli is in full tantrum. The first boss fight ensues and the player recruits Eli. They all return to the rooftop.

First Cyborg Encounter

The next day, the trio attempts to seek the message source. The journey is interrupted by the first fight with the cyborg gang. Afterwards, the team is stuck at the pier hiding out.

The Caves

A second message from the unknown source finds them at the pier. They take a boat to the beach on another side of the mountain and make their way through the caves to the forest. In the caves they discover a benevolent but pitiless mycelial entity—one that wants them to succeed but will test their mettle. Because of its primordial nature it cannot speak, and Eli has to recognize its intentions.

Aya's Trial

Emerging from the caves to the forest, the player is unknowingly at the beginning of Aya's incarnate Trauma boss's "Dungeon." Navigating to the source of the message gets the party split up by accident and Aya is left alone. Permanent Choice: All dialog choices before hand prove to have equipped her to handle this or fail and get rescued. Only one outcome is possible and once it happens, it's permanent.

The Heartolith

Aya rejoins the other two and they find the Heartolith—a conglomerate of ancient (from 2020) machines that form some type of omnipotent mind or AI, powered by mycelial networks. The Heartolith, with holographic images, shows them piece by piece the answers they've discovered through their efforts, ending with a hint of what to do next.

Eli's Shadow

Eli, troubled with knowing somehow exactly what the Heartolith wants of them and unable to share this information, leaves the rooftop hideout one night and meets a shadow boy that looks just like him. The shadow boy and Eli engage in magical play, something like Eli's tantrum boss fight but fun. This will become his Trauma Incarnate, but not tonight—tonight he gains a piece of a new way to communicate.

Eli's Capture

The cyborgs capture Eli. Luto and Aya infiltrate or frontally assault the gang HQ and are thwarted. Major Crossroads: Aya may or may not be captured as well depending on player action. Having the team split entirely up or having Luto and Aya together to rescue Eli will take some more thought and consideration.

Game Design Document

Core Concept

<3CX (Heart-Core) is a narrative-driven action RPG about recovery, resilience, and community healing. The story follows three protagonists—Luto, Aya, and Eli—whose intertwined struggles against a trauma-harvesting cyborg gang illuminate how exploitative systems feed on human suffering.

Core Mechanics

Each character embodies a hybridized RPG class whose mechanics directly mirror their psychological survival strategies:

Eli (Black Mage / Blue Mage)

  • Backstory: A nonverbal survivor of a house fire that claimed his family and left him disfigured
  • Mechanic: Spells are painted into existence using his sketchbook; he can mimic enemy abilities
  • Unique Feature: Only character capable of reversing mycelial energy extraction, freeing the planet's healing systems from exploitation

Aya (White Mage / Bard)

  • Backstory: A former search-and-rescue trainee and athlete, permanently altered by a cyborg assault
  • Mechanic: Combines first aid, chants, and performance-based buffs to heal and inspire allies
  • Arc: Struggles with self-concealment and limited trust; recovery involves nurturing both others and herself

Luto (Paladin / Berserker)

  • Backstory: A homeless youth abandoned by his family, who survived through charm and optimism
  • Mechanic: Emotional volatility system—alternates between devastating rage attacks and immobilizing remorse
  • Arc: Multiple endings; optimal resolution requires Aya's and Eli's growth to be complete

Trust-Based Combat System

The revolutionary ABS (Action Battle System) meter fills based on healing progression and current trust levels rather than arbitrary timers. When filled, Luto can call out commands to party members. Response time and effectiveness depend on:

  • Current trust level with that party member
  • The party member's healing progression
  • The type of ability being called upon
  • The party member's current emotional state

Gameplay Evolution

The game begins with stealth and exploration mechanics as isolated characters survive through avoidance. As the party bonds and heals together, gameplay shifts toward direct confrontation, mechanically reflecting the journey from individual survival to collective resistance.

Antagonists

The cyborg gang represents exploitation at both personal and systemic levels. Their leader—a woman sustained by harvested trauma energy—uses her children as enforcers. The trauma harvesting isn't the true power source; instead, it fuels her survival from a terminal illness while the gang unknowingly depends on the exploited mycelial entity.

Themes

  • Community Healing: Individual arcs are incomplete without shared progress
  • Acceptance of Loss: Luto's best resolution involves embracing self-worth without external validation
  • Reclamation: Eli's power embodies not just resistance, but transformation
  • Planetary Recovery: The mycelial entity represents healing on a global scale

Equipment System

Equipment follows street-appropriate aesthetics with clear mechanical benefits:

  • Leather jacket over plaid vest: +5 DEF
  • Metal-reinforced high-tops and gloves: +5 ATK
  • Basketball shoes: +5 AGI
  • Kung Fu headband: ABS Speed+
  • Elemental punk rock patches (subtle, grounded in reality)
