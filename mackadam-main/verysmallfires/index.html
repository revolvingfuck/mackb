ABOUT THE AUTHOR:
Robert J. Mack is a writer and game developer from Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, now based in Vancouver, BC. He holds a diploma in Graphic Design Technology. His fiction draws from Maritime life, labor, housing instability, and the particular violence of being unseen by systems designed to look like care. He makes music under the name No One. His primary game project is Heartcore. Very Small Fires is his ongoing fiction collection.
